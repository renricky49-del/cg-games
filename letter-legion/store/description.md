## Long description
In the cheerful kingdom of Letterland every soldier is a living **letter** — and baby **Prince Pip** needs them all!

Duel a computer-controlled rival on mirrored battlefields. Spend Ink to **summon** blind-box recruits: the sword-swinging **S**, the lance-thrusting **L**, the bow-bending **B** and the galloping **K**. Drop two identical letters of the same level onto each other to **merge** them into a stronger, shinier soldier — but cells are scarce, so every keep-or-merge choice matters.

Lucky summons bring **golden hero letters**. Line them up left-to-right in one row to **spell a hero's name** — KING, MAGE, MONK, HAWK, NINJA, DRUID, TITAN, SAINT — and watch the letters fuse into a hero with a spectacular special skill: meteors, arrow storms, shadow flurries, quake shouts and more.

Outlast your rival: whoever keeps their ward safe longest wins.

**Features**
- Random-summon + merge tower defense with a word-spelling twist
- 8 heroes with unique skills, upgradable by spelling them again
- 4 hand-crafted maps: Ember Plains, Misty Marsh, Crimson Bay, Iron Gate Pass
- A 9-rival ladder of CPU opponents with their own personalities and difficulty tiers
- Weapon drops in five rarities (Common → Legendary) that power up your heroes
- Item shop with active & passive items and a limited loadout
- Daily Duel: map, seed and buff of the day, with a local personal best board
- Interactive tutorial, 1×/2× speed, full mouse and touch support

All rivals are computer-controlled (CPU). No account needed — progress is saved automatically.

## Controls
- **Mouse / touch:** click **SUMMON**, then drag recruits from the tray onto cells (or click a recruit, then a cell).
- Drag a letter onto the **same letter of the same level** to merge.
- Place **golden letters** in one row, left → right, to spell heroes.
- Drag onto the **✖ bin** to sell. Click a locked cell with a shovel to dig it.
- **Keyboard (optional):** Space = summon · 1–3 = auto-place recruit · Q / W = items · F = speed · Esc = pause
