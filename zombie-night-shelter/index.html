How to Play

  1. Grab a room before the timer runs out. Click a free apartment (or walk with WASD). Rooms with a neighbour's door close by help each other.
  2. Rest on your bed to earn scrap every second. Upgrade the bed (B) to double your income — but bigger beds need a stronger door.
  3. Click empty floor in your room to build turrets and gadgets. Turret range grows with level, so turrets near the door can also protect your neighbours.
  4. The boss zombie attacks doors. Repair (R) for a quick patch or Upgrade the door (U) to refill it and raise its max HP.
  5. The boss levels up when it smashes doors. Fallen survivors rise as zombies! Survive until dawn or kill the boss to win.

Hotkeys: B bed · R repair · U door · 1-9 build · P pause · Z zoom · Mouse wheel / pinch to zoom · drag to pan.
