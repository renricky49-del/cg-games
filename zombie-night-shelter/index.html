How to Play

  1. Grab a room before the timer runs out: click a free (green) apartment.
  2. Rest on your bed to earn scrap. Upgrade the bed to earn faster.
  3. Click the floor to build turrets and gadgets. Click them again to upgrade.
  4. Bosses smash doors: Repair yours or upgrade it before it breaks.
  5. Throw a Flashbang to stun bosses and a Molotov to burn them.
  6. Help your neighbours: click their room to send scrap or fix their door.
  7. Survive until dawn or kill every boss to win. ★★ everyone alive · ★★★ every boss dead with a neighbour still alive.

Hotkeys: Q Flashbang · E Molotov · B bed · R repair · U door · 0-9 build · P pause · Z zoom · Esc close menu · Mouse wheel / pinch to zoom · drag to pan.
